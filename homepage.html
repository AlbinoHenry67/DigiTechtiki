🔄 Rotation

Understanding where players stand and how they rotate.

What is a rotation?

When your team wins the right to serve from the other team, everyone rotates one position clockwise.

Front Row

Positions 4, 3 and 2 are the front row. These players can attack above the net and block.

Back Row

Positions 5, 6 and 1 are the back row. Back-row players cannot block or attack the ball above the height of the net from in front of the attack line.

🔥 Spiking

How to approach, jump and hit the ball with power.

1. The Approach

  • Start behind the ball.
  • Use a controlled approach.
  • For a right-handed hitter, a common approach is left-right-left.
  • Build speed toward the net.

2. Jump

  • Plant both feet.
  • Swing your arms backwards.
  • Explode upwards.
  • Keep your body balanced.

3. Arm Swing

  • Pull your hitting arm back.
  • Keep your elbow high.
  • Contact the top half of the ball.
  • Snap your wrist over the ball.

4. Landing

  • Land on both feet.
  • Bend your knees.
  • Keep your chest controlled.
  • Avoid landing underneath the net.
💡 Power isn't everything. A smart hitter can score by using angles, tips, roll shots and hitting around the block instead of always trying to hit as hard as possible.

🎯 Serving

Start the rally with a consistent and difficult serve.

Float Serve

A float serve has little spin, causing the ball to move unpredictably through the air.


  • High toss control
  • Firm hand contact
  • Little or no spin
  • Follow through toward your target

Jump Serve

A jump serve combines a toss, approach and powerful contact to create a faster and more aggressive serve.


  • Consistent toss
  • Controlled approach
  • Strong jump
  • Contact at maximum reach

Serving Strategy

  • Target weaker passers.
  • Serve deep when possible.
  • Change locations.
  • Don't sacrifice consistency for power.

🛡️ Passing

A good pass gives your setter options.

Platform

Put your arms together to create a flat platform. Keep your shoulders facing your target.

Move Your Feet

Don't reach for the ball if you can move your body underneath it. Get your platform behind the ball.

Control

Use your legs and body position to control the ball. Avoid wildly swinging your arms.

🧱 Blocking

Stop attacks and control where the hitter can attack.

Read the Setter

Watch the setter and identify where the ball is going before committing to your block.

Move Quickly

Use quick lateral steps to get your body in front of the attacker.

Hands Over

Press your hands over the net rather than simply jumping straight up.

Close the Block

Work with your teammate to remove attacking angles and force the hitter toward your defenders.
